# Gulf Horizon Bank — Liquidity Risk Policy

> Synthetic document created for the Raqib hackathon demo. Parameters follow the Basel Committee's published LCR standard in simplified form. They are illustrative only.

## 1. Objective
To ensure the Bank holds enough unencumbered high-quality liquid assets (HQLA) to survive a 30-day severe liquidity stress, in line with the Basel III Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) as adopted by the Central Bank of the UAE.

## 2. Definition
LCR = Stock of HQLA ÷ Total net cash outflows over the next 30 calendar days.

## 3. HQLA
| Level | Examples | Haircut |
|---|---|---|
| Level 1 | Cash, central bank reserves, 0% risk-weight sovereign securities | 0% |
| Level 2A | 20% risk-weight sovereign and PSE securities, high-grade covered bonds | 15% |
| Level 2B | Lower-rated corporate bonds, qualifying equities | 50% |

**Caps:** Level 2B ≤ 15% of total HQLA; Level 2 (A + B) ≤ 40% of total HQLA. The caps are applied after haircuts.

## 4. Thresholds and escalation
- **Regulatory minimum:** LCR ≥ **100%** at all times.
- **Internal early-warning trigger:** LCR < **110%**. The Treasurer must notify ALCO the same day, explain the drivers, and present a remediation plan (for example, raising term funding or converting Level 2 assets to Level 1).
- **Breach (< 100%):** immediate notification to the CEO, the Board Risk Committee and the regulator, and activation of the Contingency Funding Plan.

## 5. Run-off and inflow rates (30-day stress)
| Category | Rate |
|---|---|
| Retail deposits — stable | 5% |
| Retail deposits — less stable | 10% |
| Wholesale — operational deposits | 25% |
| Wholesale — non-operational, non-financial corporates | 40% |
| Funding from financial institutions | 100% |
| Committed credit and liquidity facilities (undrawn) | 10% |
| Contractual inflows | Counted up to 75% of gross outflows |

## 6. Reporting
The LCR is calculated daily by Treasury and reported monthly to the regulator. The daily LCR series, its drivers, and any early-warning events must be available to Risk and Compliance on demand.

## 7. Concentration
No single depositor group may exceed 5% of total deposits without ALCO approval. Large corporate withdrawals are the most common driver of LCR volatility.
