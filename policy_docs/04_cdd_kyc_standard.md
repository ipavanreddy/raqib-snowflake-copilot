# Gulf Horizon Bank — CDD / KYC Standard

> Synthetic document created for the Raqib hackathon demo. Gulf Horizon Bank is fictional.

## 1. Identification and verification
1.1 **Individuals:** a valid Emirates ID (residents) or passport and visa (non-residents), proof of address, and occupation and employer details.
1.2 **Legal entities:** trade licence, memorandum of association, register of beneficial owners (25% or more ownership or control), and identification of authorised signatories.

## 2. Beneficial ownership
The Bank identifies and verifies every natural person who ultimately owns or controls 25% or more of a legal entity, or who otherwise exercises control. Where none can be identified, the senior managing official is recorded.

## 3. Customer risk rating
Customers are rated LOW, MEDIUM or HIGH using the following factors: customer type and occupation or industry; PEP status; nationality and residence; products and channels; expected activity; and adverse media.
Customers in the following industries are rated at least MEDIUM: precious metals and stones, real estate, money exchange, general trading with cross-border flows, and auto trading.

## 4. Enhanced due diligence (EDD)
Mandatory for HIGH-risk customers and PEPs: source of wealth and source of funds evidence; senior management approval; and enhanced monitoring.

## 5. Periodic review
| Risk rating | Maximum review interval |
|---|---|
| HIGH | 12 months |
| MEDIUM | 24 months |
| LOW | 36 months |

A review is **overdue** when the time since the last review exceeds the maximum interval. Overdue reviews increase the customer risk score (TM Rulebook s.6) and must be cleared within 30 days.

## 6. Expected activity profile
6.1 At onboarding, every customer declares an **expected monthly turnover** (AED), the expected transaction types, and the main counterparty countries.
6.2 Monitoring compares actual behaviour with this profile. Third-party and cash credits over any 30 days of at least **5 times** the declared turnover (and at least AED 50,000) raise TM-07.
6.3 If the deviation has a legitimate explanation, the relationship manager updates the profile with supporting documents. Unexplained deviations are escalated.

## 7. Trigger events
A KYC refresh is required on: a material change in activity; adverse media; an STR being filed; a change of ownership; or reactivation of a dormant account (180 or more days without activity).
