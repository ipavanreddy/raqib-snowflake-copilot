# Gulf Horizon Bank — STR Filing Guide

> Synthetic document created for the Raqib hackathon demo. The structure is modelled on common goAML report fields and is illustrative only.

## 1. When to file
An STR is filed when the MLRO knows, suspects, or has reasonable grounds to suspect that funds are the proceeds of crime or are linked to ML, TF, PF or an illegal organisation. Certainty is not required. The STR must be filed **without delay** once suspicion is formed, and before any further transaction is executed where possible.

## 2. Report structure
Every STR must contain the following sections:
1. **Reporting entity:** Gulf Horizon Bank, MLRO name, internal case reference.
2. **Report type and reason:** STR (suspicious transaction) or SAR (suspicious activity without a transaction), plus the primary indicator, for example structuring or a sanctions near-match.
3. **Subject(s):** full name, customer ID, ID document number (masked in internal drafts), nationality, occupation or industry, address, accounts (IBAN), customer since, and KYC risk rating.
4. **Transactions:** list of transactions relied on, with date, amount, currency, AED equivalent, channel, counterparty, counterparty country and branch.
5. **Grounds for suspicion (narrative):** a factual, chronological account answering *who, what, when, where, why and how*. It must:
   - describe the pattern and why it is unusual for this customer (compared with the KYC profile);
   - reference the detection rule(s) and the policy clauses breached or triggered;
   - summarise the analyst's investigation steps and any explanations received;
   - avoid speculation, opinion about guilt, and unexplained jargon.
6. **Red-flag indicators:** a bullet list mapped to typologies.
7. **Action taken:** for example account restricted, relationship under review, funds frozen (sanctions), or a recall request sent.
8. **Attachments:** evidence references (statements, KYC documents, screening results).

## 3. Quality checklist
- Every amount in the narrative must match the transaction list.
- Every claim must be supported by evidence in the case file.
- Dates use the format YYYY-MM-DD; amounts are in AED with the original currency shown.
- No tipping-off: the customer must not learn of the report.
- The MLRO approves the final text. An analyst may draft, but only the MLRO files.

## 4. After filing
- Record the goAML reference number in the case.
- Apply enhanced monitoring to the subject for 6 months.
- Review the relationship; exit decisions must not tip off the customer.
- Respond to any FIU request for information within the deadline it sets.

## 5. Records
STRs and all supporting evidence are kept for at least five years and are accessible only to Compliance roles.
